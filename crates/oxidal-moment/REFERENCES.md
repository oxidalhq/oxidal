# Sources and references — Oxidal Moment

Written for Oxidal from the published descriptions named here and from first
principles. No third-party source code was copied, ported or used as a base,
and no commercial unit was measured or fitted. See
[the clean code rule](../../docs/native-effects.md).

The sources, by the short names used in the table:

- **Resampling**: [Smith, "Digital Audio Resampling Home Page"](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~jos/resample/)
- **DAFX**: [Zölzer (ed.), "DAFX: Digital Audio Effects", 2nd edition, 2011](https://doi.org/10.1002/9781119991298)
- **Microsound**: [Roads, "Microsound", MIT Press, 2001; paperback 2004](https://mitpressbookstore.mit.edu/book/9780262681544)
- **Catmull-Rom**: [Catmull and Rom, "A Class of Local Interpolating Splines", 1974](https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-0-12-079050-0.50020-5)
- **Schroeder**: [Schroeder, "Natural Sounding Artificial Reverberation", JAES, 1962](https://aes2.org/publications/elibrary-page/?id=849)
- **Dattorro 1**: [Dattorro, "Effect Design, Part 1: Reverberator and Other Filters", JAES, 1997](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~dattorro/EffectDesignPart1.pdf)
- **Dattorro 2**: [Dattorro, "Effect Design, Part 2: Delay-Line Modulation and Chorus", JAES, 1997](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~dattorro/EffectDesignPart2.pdf)
- **Compressor**: [Giannoulis, Massberg and Reiss, "Digital Dynamic Range Compressor Design: A Tutorial and Analysis", JAES, 2012](https://aes2.org/publications/elibrary-page/?id=16354)
- **Xorshift**: [Marsaglia, "Xorshift RNGs", Journal of Statistical Software, 2003](https://doi.org/10.18637/jss.v008.i14)
- **Butterworth**: [Smith, "Butterworth Lowpass Design", Introduction to Digital Filters](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~jos/filters/Butterworth_Lowpass_Design.html)
- **Overlap-add**: [Smith, "Overlap-Add (OLA) STFT Processing", Spectral Audio Signal Processing](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~jos/sasp/Overlap_Add_OLA_STFT_Processing.html)
- **All-pass sections**: [Smith, "Schroeder Allpass Sections", Physical Audio Signal Processing](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~jos/pasp/Schroeder_Allpass_Sections.html)

| Part                     | What it is                                                                   | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | How its constants were chosen                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Clock` rate reduction   | Low-pass, keep one sample per step, join the steps by a line, low-pass again | [Resampling](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~jos/resample/); [DAFX](https://doi.org/10.1002/9781119991298), sampling rate conversion; [Butterworth](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~jos/filters/Butterworth_Lowpass_Design.html) for the filter stages                | Corner at 0.45 of the working rate. Stage Q values 0.5412 and 1.3066 are the fourth-order Butterworth values, `1 / (2 cos(π/8))` and `1 / (2 cos(3π/8))`                                    |
| `CLOCK_RATIOS`           | The working rates Steps moves through                                        | First principles                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Octaves (2, 4, 8, 16) and the fifths between them (1.5, 3, 6, 12), so a playing loop moves by musical intervals                                                                             |
| Grit                     | Crossfades both Clock filters away, leaving a held signal                    | First principles: a sample-and-hold has no anti-aliasing or rebuild filter                                                                                                                                                                                    | A plain crossfade                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `Memory::read`           | Four-point read between samples                                              | [Catmull-Rom](https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-0-12-079050-0.50020-5), from its definition                                                                                                                                                                        | None                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `knee`                   | Limits everything stored in a memory                                         | First principles                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Unity to 0.9, then `u / (1 + u)`, whose slope at the start of the bend is 1, so there is no corner                                                                                          |
| Loop join                | Crossfades into the audio from before the loop's start                       | [DAFX](https://doi.org/10.1002/9781119991298), delay-based effects (crossfaded reads of one delay memory)                                                                                                                                                     | At most 256 steps or a quarter of the loop, chosen by ear                                                                                                                                   |
| Tape                     | A read position moved at a speed ratio                                       | [DAFX](https://doi.org/10.1002/9781119991298), delay-based effects                                                                                                                                                                                            | `TAPE_SPEEDS`: octaves from half to four times, either way                                                                                                                                  |
| Stretch and Touch slices | Two overlapping slices with raised-cosine windows that sum to one            | [Microsound](https://mitpressbookstore.mit.edu/book/9780262681544) (granulation); [DAFX](https://doi.org/10.1002/9781119991298), time stretching by overlap and add; [Overlap-add](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~jos/sasp/Overlap_Add_OLA_STFT_Processing.html) | Slices 20 ms to 500 ms. Stretch's speed is the square of Move's distance from the middle, so the slow settings get most of the knob                                                         |
| Touch level detector     | Peak follower with attack and release, with hysteresis                       | [Compressor](https://aes2.org/publications/elibrary-page/?id=16354) (level detection)                                                                                                                                                                         | 2 ms attack, 80 ms release; lets go at half the threshold. Threshold -6 dB to -60 dB                                                                                                        |
| Overdub and Fade         | The stored sample scaled by Fade, plus the input                             | [DAFX](https://doi.org/10.1002/9781119991298), feedback delay                                                                                                                                                                                                 | None                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Smear diffusers          | Four delay-based all-pass stages                                             | [Schroeder](https://aes2.org/publications/elibrary-page/?id=849); [Dattorro 1](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~dattorro/EffectDesignPart1.pdf); [All-pass sections](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~jos/pasp/Schroeder_Allpass_Sections.html)                         | Lengths 0.034, 0.055, 0.089 and 0.144 of the size: ratios of Fibonacci numbers, so they share no common period. Gain up to 0.72, chosen by ear below the 0.75 of Dattorro 1                 |
| Smear taps               | Five output taps                                                             | [Schroeder](https://aes2.org/publications/elibrary-page/?id=849) (multi-tap echoes)                                                                                                                                                                           | Places 0.090, 0.236, 0.472, 0.618 and 0.854: the fractional parts of the first five multiples of the golden ratio, sorted. Gains fall by half across the size and are scaled to unit energy |
| Delay                    | One delay with feedback; a time change crossfades two read positions         | [Dattorro 2](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~dattorro/EffectDesignPart2.pdf)                                                                                                                                                                                      | 1024 steps of crossfade, chosen by ear                                                                                                                                                      |
| Slip                     | Two crossfaded read positions sliding through a short memory                 | [DAFX](https://doi.org/10.1002/9781119991298), delay-line pitch shifting                                                                                                                                                                                      | Raised-cosine windows; semitone steps of `2^(n/12)`                                                                                                                                         |
| Chance                   | One random decision per Time period opens the input                          | [Xorshift](https://doi.org/10.18637/jss.v008.i14), through `oxidal_dsp::Noise`                                                                                                                                                                                | A 64-step gate, so the opening does not click                                                                                                                                               |
| Tone                     | One-pole high cut                                                            | `oxidal_dsp::OnePole`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | 300 Hz to 20 kHz, exponential                                                                                                                                                               |
