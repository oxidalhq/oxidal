# Sources and references — Oxidal Sustain

Written for Oxidal from the published descriptions named here and from Oxidal's own earlier code. No third-party source code was copied, ported or used as a base. See [the clean code rule](../../docs/native-effects.md).

| Part                                      | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Constants                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Pitch reading (`pitch.rs`, `PitchReader`) | Cumulative mean normalised difference function, first dip under a threshold, parabolic refinement; the difference is taken over the newest samples (the paper's d_t(τ) with t at the end of the frame) so a moving pitch is read with little delay | de Cheveigné and Kawahara (2002) [1], steps 2 to 5; rewritten from Oxidal's tuner (`crates/oxidal-audio/src/tuner.rs`)                                                                                   | Analysis rate about 8 kHz as in the tuner; window 192 and longest lag 200 samples from the range 40 Hz to 1.6 kHz; hop 10 ms; threshold 0.15 from the paper's step 4, fallback 0.3 chosen here                                       |
| Refinement over several periods           | The dip at a multiple of the period divides the error; the distance is doubled each step, and limited to 12.5 ms so a moving pitch is not averaged                                                                                                 | Oxidal's tuner, extended here                                                                                                                                                                            | Search of ±2 samples and a tolerance that halves with each doubling, derived from the error of the previous step                                                                                                                     |
| Single-note check                         | Hann-windowed sums at harmonics 1, 7, 11 and 13 of the period found                                                                                                                                                                                | Discrete Fourier transform at one frequency, textbook; window from Harris (1978) [2]. Choice of harmonics derived here: they are absent from the common period of any two notes a third to a sixth apart | Share 1.5 % of frame power: above the window's −31 dB side lobes                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Pitch guard (`PitchGuard`)                | Agreement of three readings, median of three for jumps (smooth steps under 30 cents with confidence 0.9 taken directly), hold on unclear readings, delay for octave-related jumps, a follow window that widens after a gap                         | Median smoothing of pitch tracks: Rabiner, Sambur and Schmidt (1975) [3]. The rest is Oxidal's own logic                                                                                                 | 60 cents per reading (faster than a bend), 40 cents agreement, 150 ms for octave errors: chosen here                                                                                                                                 |
| Low-level tracking                        | Automatic gain in front of the pitch reader; stricter confidence, a steady-level check and a 300 ms lose/regain hysteresis for a faint string                                                                                                      | Gain computer as in the dynamics row; the relation between the dip depth and the signal-to-noise ratio follows from the definition of the normalised difference (noise adds its power to every lag)      | Gain up to +60 dB toward −20 dBFS, 100 ms recovery; confidence 0.95 (about 13 dB SNR); halves within 6 dB: chosen here                                                                                                               |
| Smooth retuning                           | Linear move to each reading over one hop, one-step prediction along a steady movement, short glide                                                                                                                                                 | Linear interpolation and first-order extrapolation, from their definitions                                                                                                                               | Prediction 0.75 of the last step, only for two like steps under 35 cents with confidence 0.98; glide 4 ms: chosen here and checked by the follow tests                                                                               |
| Pick detection                            | Fast follower against a slow one, hold-off and re-arming                                                                                                                                                                                           | Oxidal Swell (`native/swell`)                                                                                                                                                                            | Swell's 50 ms hold-off; thresholds mapped from Sensitivity here                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Mute detection                            | Fast follower falling far below the slow one                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Derived here: for a level falling at r nepers a second, followers with times a and b settle at a ratio (1 − r·b)/(1 − r·a)                                                                               | Drop 18 to 8 dB and slow time 60 to 200 ms, giving limits of 130 to 27 dB a second                                                                                                                                                   |
| Lift                                      | Slow feed-forward gain toward a target, with a ceiling and a noise-floor fade                                                                                                                                                                      | Gain computer and smoothing as in Giannoulis, Massberg and Reiss (2012) [4]                                                                                                                              | Ceiling 12 to 3 dB from Drive; 250 ms rise, 20 ms fall: chosen here                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Driver loop (`driver.rs`)                 | Delay line with feedback tuned to the period, excited by the input (comb filter, waveguide string)                                                                                                                                                 | Karplus and Strong (1983) [5]; Jaffe and Smith (1983) [6]; Smith, _Physical Audio Signal Processing_ [7], chapters on comb filters and the extended Karplus-Strong algorithm                             | Injection 0.25 from the wanted memory of four periods at full level; fractional delay by `oxidal_dsp::DelayLine::read_cubic`                                                                                                         |
| Loop damping                              | Three-point symmetric smoothing, gain 1 − 4c·sin²(ω/2), one sample of delay                                                                                                                                                                        | Length-three symmetric loop filter in Smith, _Physical Audio Signal Processing_ [7]; decay-rate reasoning from Jaffe and Smith (1983) [6]                                                                | c set so that 3 kHz fades 1 dB a second at any pitch: c = rate·P/(fs·ω²), derived in the code                                                                                                                                        |
| Limiter in the loop                       | Loop gain set from the measured level: ln(gain) proportional to ln(target/level)                                                                                                                                                                   | Automatic gain control as a first-order loop in the logarithm of the level, derived here; level detector is two one-pole averages                                                                        | Growth time 60 to 400 ms from Drive; pull-down exponent 0.3 and margin 0.5 dB from the phase margin with the detector's lag; growth cap 60 dB a second                                                                               |
| Offset removal in the loop                | One-pole high-pass at 0.5 Hz with its phase lead undone by lengthening the delay                                                                                                                                                                   | First-order filter, textbook; lead of fc/f radians gives fc·P²/(2π·fs) samples, derived in the code                                                                                                      | 0.5 Hz: faster than any offset can grow, under 2 cents of stretch between harmonics                                                                                                                                                  |
| Level plan (`engine.rs`)                  | Held level from the note's peak; voice fills what the lifted string lacks, by amplitude in step or by power for a harmonic                                                                                                                         | Derived here; equal-power law from `oxidal_dsp::equal_power`                                                                                                                                             | Plateau −9 to 0 dB from Drive: chosen here                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Radix-2 FFT (`chord.rs`, `Fft`)           | Iterative decimation-in-time transform, one butterfly pass per call                                                                                                                                                                                | Cooley and Tukey (1965) [8]; any DSP textbook. Structure follows Oxidal Hold's own (`native/hold`)                                                                                                       | None                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Chord analysis                            | Two Hann frames a quarter apart, peaks of the newest, frequency from the phase turned between them                                                                                                                                                 | Oxidal Hold; the phase method is Flanagan and Golden (1966) [9] and Dolson (1986) [10]                                                                                                                   | Frame 8192 (16384 at 96 kHz) so notes 23 Hz apart separate; peak rules (largest within two bins, 6 dB fall across the lobe, within 30 dB of the top) from the Hann main lobe; amplitude corrected by the lobe shape sinc(d)/(1 − d²) |
| Chord resynthesis                         | One sinusoid per peak, each a rotated unit vector                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Sinusoidal model: McAulay and Quatieri (1986) [11]; oscillator by rotation, from the definition                                                                                                          | 32 partials; unit length restored every 1024 samples by one Newton step                                                                                                                                                              |
| Tone, input and voice high-pass           | State-variable low-pass, one-pole filters                                                                                                                                                                                                          | `oxidal-dsp` (see its record)                                                                                                                                                                            | 400 Hz to 8 kHz; 30 Hz and 20 Hz corners                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Entry point                               | `oxidal_lv2_plugin::export!`                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Oxidal's plugin interface crate                                                                                                                                                                          | None                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
