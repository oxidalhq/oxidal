# Sources and references — Oxidal Motion

Written for Oxidal from the published descriptions named here and from first
principles. No third-party source code was copied, ported or used as a base,
and no value was taken from a commercial unit, its schematic or a measurement
of it. See [the clean code rule](../../docs/native-effects.md).

Sources, by short name:

- **Dattorro 1997** [1]: J. Dattorro, "Effect Design, Part 2: Delay-Line
  Modulation and Chorus", _Journal of the Audio Engineering Society_ 45(10).
- **DAFX** [2]: U. Zölzer (ed.), _DAFX: Digital Audio Effects_, 2nd edition,
  Wiley, 2011: the chapters on modulators and on delay-based effects.
- **Smith, PASP** [3]: J. O. Smith, _Physical Audio Signal Processing_, the
  sections on flanging, phasing with first-order all-pass filters and digital
  sinusoid generators.
- **Rotary paper** [4]: J. Smith, S. Serafin, J. Abel and D. Berners, on Doppler
  simulation and the rotary speaker, _Proc. 5th Int. Conf. on Digital Audio
  Effects (DAFx-02)_, Hamburg, 2002.
- **Vibe paper** [5]: C. Darabundit, R. Wedelich and P. Bischoff, a grey-box model
  of a lamp-and-photocell phase shifter pedal, _Proc. 22nd Int. Conf. on
  Digital Audio Effects (DAFx-19)_, Birmingham, 2019, paper 31. Used for the
  behaviour only (staggered stages; a photocell that turns on and off at
  different speeds, so the sweep grows lopsided with intensity). Its measured
  curves and circuit values are not used.
- **Linkwitz 1976** [6]: S. Linkwitz, "Active Crossover Networks for Noncoincident
  Drivers", _Journal of the Audio Engineering Society_ 24(1).
